# Aetheria — Story Summary

A living reference for the game's narrative. Player-facing lore is written into the
Adventurer's Journal (`src/domain/journal.ts`, one chapter per entry, unlocked as
stages are cleared). This file is the **behind-the-curtain** version: it records
what the player knows, what they don't yet, and where the story is going, so the
journal chapters stay consistent as they're written.

> ⚠️ **Contains spoilers the player has not learned.** The journal's Introduction
> deliberately withholds the twist below.

## The world

**Aetheria** was once the fairest and most powerful kingdom in the world — radiant
towers, clear rivers, abundant fields, renowned libraries. It was strong not
through conquest but because it was loved and at peace. This is its golden age,
the "heyday" the Introduction mourns.

## The fall

**King Kael** took the throne **two years ago**. His reign broke sharply from the
peaceful kings before him: the kingdom was suddenly gripped by a hunger to expand
and marched to war. The wars gutted Aetheria — its people, fields, rivers, and
treasuries all ruined. In two years the greatest kingdom in the world was brought
to gravely-declined ruin.

## The player — a Hero

- The player is chosen by the **Voice of the World** to be a **Hero** — one of the
  rare souls able to turn the tide of an age.
- Their power is **still developing / half-awoken**, so they keep it **secret**,
  revealing little of what they are.
- Unable to raise an army, the Hero instead assembles a **party of trusted
  adventurers** and sets out for the capital to **stop the king**.
- The march on the castle is represented in-game by the **castle section of the
  level menu** — those stages are the journey to and assault on the king.

## The twist (NOT yet known to the player)

- **King Kael is not the true villain.** He is a **puppet** — someone or something
  else is the real power behind Aetheria's sudden turn to war and decline.
- The player currently believes the king is the source of all this and is marching
  to end *him*. The revelation that Kael was controlled is a later beat, to be
  seeded and paid off in the journal chapters and stages that follow the castle.

## Narrative status / how it maps to systems

- **Journal Introduction** — written (covers the heyday, Kael's rise, the war and
  decline, the Voice, the secret power, and the party). Withholds the puppet twist.
- **Journal Chapters I+** — unlock one per stage cleared; currently unwritten.
  These are the place to develop the march, the party, hints toward the true
  villain, and eventually the reveal.
- **Castle stages** — the party's road to and confrontation with the king.
