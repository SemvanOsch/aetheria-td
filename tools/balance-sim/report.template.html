Campaign Balance

An automated bot played each stage on the real game engine with different heroes, teams and mastery levels. Each setup was played at the normal starting gold, and searched for the lowest starting gold that still wins. That number is the main yardstick here: lower means an easier stage or a stronger setup.

          How much to trust this

          The bot is a decent player, not a human. It places units where they cover the most path, spends gold greedily on whatever its damage estimate favours, lights a lantern when it gives placed champions their range back, and casts hero abilities as soon as they're ready. It wears no armor. Random seeds only change crits, dodges and Bard targets. Damage per gold also depends on how much enemy HP a stage has, so compare units within a stage rather than across stages. Trust the rankings more than the exact numbers.
